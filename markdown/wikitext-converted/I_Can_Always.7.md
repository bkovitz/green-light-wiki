Here's a possible angle on the [[Function Attitude]]s.
_Fi:_ Give me anything, and I can always find a way to see it as a one-of-a-kind living thing answering its own, unique calling.
_Te:_ Give me anything, and I can always measure it and compare it against alternatives.
_Si:_ Show me anything, and I can always see a way in which it might fail to protect you against the unpredictable.
_Ne:_ Give me anything, and I can always incorporate it into something greater.
_Fe:_ Show me anyone, and I can always tell you where their loyalties lie and how they're showing each other that they've cast their lot together.
_Ti:_ Let me get my hands on anything, and I can always figure out how it works.
_Se:_ I can't tell you anything until you show me.  And then I'll know everything there is to know, because it'll be obvious.
_Ni:_ Let me see you look at anything, and I'll see how the way in which you're looking at it is distorting your perception of the real thing.
