[[Extraverted Thinking]] as their [[Dominant Function]] [[Introverted Intuition]] as their [[Secondary Function]] [[Extraverted Sensation]] as their [[Tertiary Function]] [[Introverted Feeling]] as their [[Inferior Function]]
