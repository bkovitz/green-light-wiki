Here's a possible angle on the [[Function Attitude]]s.
 - Fi: Give me anything, and I can always find a way to see it as a one-of-a-kind living thing answering its own, unique calling.
 - Te: Give me anything, and I can always measure it and compare it against alternatives.
 - Si: Show me anything, and I can always see a way in which it might fail to protect you against the unpredictable.
 - Ne: Give me anything, and I can always incorporate it into something greater.
 - Fe: Show me anyone, and I can always tell you where their loyalties lie and how they're showing each other that they've cast their lot together.
 - Ti: Let me get my hands on anything, and I can always figure out how it works.
 - Se: I can't tell you anything until you show me.  And then I'll know everything there is to know, because it'll be obvious.
 - Ni: Show me anything, and I can always see through it to the _real_ reality that lurks behind.
