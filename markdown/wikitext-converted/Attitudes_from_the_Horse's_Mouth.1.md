Relating to [[Type from Scratch]], here are some observations about finding descriptions of [[function attitude]]s by the people whose sense of self takes shape through them.  This might be among the most direct evidence for the existence of [[function attitude]]s.
##  First, a difficulty: the invisible dominant 
When people read [[Lenore]]'s descriptions of types and attitudes, even the attempts at [[exegesis]] posted on this site, they seldom recognize themselves in their [[dominant function]].  The reaction is usually something like, "Whah?  Maybe sorta kinda, but this hardly seems like."
