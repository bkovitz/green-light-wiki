An alternate or auxiliary [[Function Attitude]] in addition to one's [[Dominant Function]].  The [[Secondary Function]] gives extraverts access to something beyond what is immediately apparent or applicable; and it gives introverts access to public, worldly matters.
One of [[Lenore]]'s main ideas seems to be that the [[Secondary Function]] often provides a person's main path of growth, once they've established a definite personality--one strong enough to start running into self-created limitations.
