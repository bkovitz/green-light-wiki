In [[Lenore Thomson]]'s theory, the four-letter code "ENFP" stands for a person with:
[[Extraverted Intuition]] as their [[Dominant Function]] [[Introverted Feeling]] as their [[Secondary Function]] [[Extraverted Thinking]] as their [[Tertiary Function]] [[Introverted Sensation]] as their [[Inferior Function]]
