Ti: Affirming the consequent (the harmony that I see is the only harmony possible).
Te: Straw man, false dichotomy.
Se: _Non sequitur_ (stark raving illogic), _ad baculum._
Fe: _Ad verecundiam._
Ni: _Ad ignorantiam,_ equivocation, "Some people might disagree."
Fi: _Ad misericordiam._
Ne: Equivocation?
Si: Appeal to tradition?
All the extraverted attitudes favor _ad hominem._
