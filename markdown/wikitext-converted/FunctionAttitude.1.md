A _function-attitude_ is a Jungian function used in an [[Extraverted]] or [[Introverted]] attitude.
There are four functions:
[[Sensation]] [[Intuition]] [[Thinking]] [[Feeling]]
That makes eight function-attitudes:
[[Extraverted Sensation]] [[Extraverted Intuition]] [[Extraverted Thinking]] [[Extraverted Feeling]] [[Introverted Sensation]] [[Introverted Intuition]] [[Introverted Thinking]] [[Introverted Feeling]]
[[Lenore]] often refers to function-attitudes just as "functions".
On this [[wiki http://greenlightwiki.com/lenore-exegesis]], we learn toward the more intuitively clear word "attitudes".
