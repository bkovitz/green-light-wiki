[[Introverted Thinking]] as their [[Dominant Function]] [[Extraverted Intuition]] as their [[Secondary Function]] [[Introverted Sensation]] as their [[Tertiary Function]] [[Extraverted Feeling]] as their [[Inferior Function]]
