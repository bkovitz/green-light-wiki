Here are some other web sites that contain material of relevance to [[Lenore Thomson]] and personality type.
##  Sites with detailed [[function attitude]] theories 
Henry L. "Dick" Thompson's "High-Performing Systems" web site: http://www.hpsys.com/
Henry is the author of _[[Jung's Function-Attitudes Explained http://www.hpsys.com/jfarev.htm]]._
Team Technology, run by Steve Myers, inventor of the Mental Muscle Diagram: http://www.teamtechnology.co.uk/tt/t-articl/mb-dynam.htm
##  Sites with a more classically [[Myers-Briggs]]ian "be true to your type" approach 
Tieger & Tieger's site: http://www.personalitytype.com/
[[David Keirsey]]'s site: http://keirsey.com/
The Center for Applications of Psychological Type, the official [[Myers-Briggs]] site (includes some [[function attitude]] stuff: http://capt.org/
16types.com, aimed at type practitioners: http://www.16types.com/
Kathleen Benziger, Ph.D.  All about "falsifying type" as the root of unhappiness: http://www.benziger.org/
##  Hard to categorize 
Joe Butt's site: http://www.typelogic.com/
Brenda Muller's Personality Page (observationally rich profiles, concrete and straightforward guidance for personal growth for each time: [[Lenore]]-like in its emphasis on growth, un-[[Lenore]]-like in its writing style): http://www.personalitypage.com/
##  Enneagram 
Riso and Hudson's site: http://www.enneagraminstitute.com/
9Types.com http://www.9types.com/
The Enneagram and the MBTI: An Electronic Journal. Edited by Patricia Dinkelaker and John Fudjack. Possibly very [[Lenore]]-relevant, even includes [[a conversation with Lenore http://tap3x.net/EMBTI/j2dialogues.html#lenore]]: http://tap3x.net/EMBTI/journal.html
##  Wild sites (some might say "crackpot" sites) 
The controversial Socionics site, with its own radical take on [[function attitude]]s: http://www.socionics.com/
PTypes: linking each [[Myers-Briggs]] type to a personality disorder, and offering stoicism as a path to growth for each type: http://www.geocities.com/ptypes/
