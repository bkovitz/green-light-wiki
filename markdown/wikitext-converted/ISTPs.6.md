In [[Lenore Thomson]]'s theory, the four-letter code "ISTP" stands for a person with:
[[Introverted Thinking]] as their [[Dominant Function]] [[Extraverted Sensation]] as their [[Secondary Function]] [[Introverted Intuition]] as their [[Tertiary Function]] [[Extraverted Feeling]] as their [[Inferior Function]]
