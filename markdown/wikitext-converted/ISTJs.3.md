In [[Lenore Thomson]]'s theory, the four-letter code "ISTJ" stands for a person with:
[[Introverted Sensation]] as their [[Dominant Function]] [[Extraverted Thinking]] as their [[Secondary Function]] [[Introverted Feeling]] as their [[Tertiary Function]] [[Extraverted Intuition]] as their [[Inferior Function]]
